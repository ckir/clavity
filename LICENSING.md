partner one. The public licence is the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0, which makes the software
source-available. It is not open source.

|---|---|---|---|
| An individual, school, charity or other noncommercial user | Public licence (PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0) | [LICENSE](LICENSE) | Nothing. Use it under the terms of that file. |
| An organisation trying clavity out before deciding | Public licence plus its 30-day Commercial Evaluation Grant | The additional terms at the end of [LICENSE](LICENSE) | Nothing for the first 30 days, which run from your first use and are royalty-free. After that, and for any production use, an organisation that is not a noncommercial one needs a commercial licence (next row). |
| A business or other organisation using clavity commercially | Commercial licence | [licenses/COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md](licenses/COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md) (a template) | Write to the contact address below. The licence exists once both sides have signed it. |
| A trainer or training company that wants to run paid seminars about clavity | Authorized Training Partner licence | [licenses/TRAINING-PARTNER-LICENSE.md](licenses/TRAINING-PARTNER-LICENSE.md) (a template) | Write to the contact address below. The licence exists once both sides have signed it. |
| A person attending a seminar run by an Authorized Training Partner | Covered for the seminar by the partner's agreement | Schedule B of the training partner licence | Nothing beyond what the partner asks you to accept. After the seminar the public licence applies to you. |

## Paid licences

Only the copyright holder grants a commercial or a training partner licence, in writing, to a named
party. The files in `licenses/` are templates: they grant nothing by themselves, and reading, copying or
acting on one does not create a licence. They have not yet been reviewed by legal counsel.

## Authorized Clavity Training Partners

The title "Authorized Clavity Training Partner" may be used only by a party that holds a signed training
partner agreement that is in force. To check whether a named trainer is authorized, write to the contact
