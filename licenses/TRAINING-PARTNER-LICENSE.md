## 2. Definitions

- **Software**: clavity and the products in the repository at https://github.com/ckir/clavity (clavity-dotnet,
  clavity-classic, agy-autotrain, commonmemory, review-relay), with their documentation.
- **Public Licence**: the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0 and the additional terms in the file
  `LICENSE` of the Software.
- **Event**: any instructional event about the Software that the Partner or its Affiliates run or advertise.
- **Seminar**: an Event held in person or online, with a start and an end date announced in advance, lasting
  no more than the maximum number of consecutive days set in Schedule A, of which the Licensor was told in
  time under section 7. An Event that is not a Seminar is dealt with in sections 5, 7 and 8.
- **Affiliate**: a person or entity that controls, is controlled by or is under common control with the
  Partner, where control means owning more than half of the voting rights or the power to direct
  management. An individual who owns or controls the Partner is an Affiliate.
- **Attendee**: a person who is registered for or who attends a specific Seminar, whether or not the
  Partner's registration record shows that person.
- **Seminar Exercises**: instructional tasks set by the Partner during a Seminar. Work on anyone's
  production code, or on a deliverable for a client, is not a Seminar Exercise.
- **Seminar Revenue**: everything the Partner or its Affiliates receive for a Seminar. When a Seminar is
  sold together with other goods or services, or given away as part of another deal, Seminar Revenue is
  the price the Partner charges for a comparable Seminar sold alone.
- **Allowed Direct Costs**: the following costs of a Seminar, each supported by a third-party receipt, and
  nothing else: venue; catering; travel and lodging for instructors; fees paid to instructors who are not
  Affiliates of the Partner; payment-processing charges; printing. Partner salaries, overhead and
  payments to Affiliates are never Allowed Direct Costs.
- **Seminar Profit**: Seminar Revenue minus Allowed Direct Costs for that Seminar, never less than zero.
  A loss on one Seminar is not carried to another.
- **Royalty Base**: the amount named as the royalty base in Schedule A: Seminar Profit, unless Schedule A
  says Seminar Revenue.

## 3. Grants

The Licensor grants the Partner the following rights, all non-exclusive and non-transferable, for the
term and the territory stated in Schedule A:

1. To use the Software to prepare and deliver Seminars.
2. To sublicense each Attendee to use the Software for Seminar Exercises on the scheduled days of the

## 5. Royalty and Payment

For each Seminar, and for each other Event whether it is paid, free or given away as part of another
deal, the Partner pays the Licensor the Royalty Percentage stated in Schedule A of the Royalty Base for
it. For an Event that is not a Seminar, the terms in this agreement that refer to a Seminar (Seminar
Revenue, Allowed Direct Costs, Seminar Profit, Attendee, the report, the minimum royalty and the audit)
apply to it as if it were a Seminar, and the people who attend it count as Attendees for the minimum
royalty. This payment obligation grants no right.

The royalty for a Seminar is never less than the Minimum Royalty per Attendee stated in Schedule A
multiplied by the number of Attendees of that Seminar.

No person may be an Attendee for more than the number of Seminar days per twelve months stated in
Schedule A, counted across all of the Partner's Seminars.

The Partner pays with the report required by section 6. Amounts are exclusive of taxes, which the
Partner bears unless the law requires otherwise.

## 6. Reports, Records and Audit

Within the number of days stated in Schedule A after a Seminar ends, the Partner sends the Licensor a
report for that Seminar: the dates, the number of Attendees, the Seminar Revenue, each Allowed Direct
Cost with its receipt, the Seminar Profit and the royalty due.

The Partner keeps the records behind each report for the retention period stated in Schedule A. The
Licensor may audit those records on written notice. The Partner pays any shortfall an audit finds. If the
shortfall is above the threshold stated in Schedule A, the Partner also bears the cost of the audit.

## 7. Partner Obligations

The Partner:

- gives no warranty on the Licensor's behalf;
- does not present a Seminar as run or endorsed by Google or Anthropic;
- tells the Licensor in writing of each Event, with its dates, at least the number of days before it

An Event of which the Licensor was not told in time is not a Seminar. The Software may not be used at it
under this agreement, no Attendee has a sublicence for it, and it is not protected by the in-flight rule in
section 8. Running such an Event is a material breach of this agreement that cannot be cured after the
Event has taken place; paying the royalty for it does not remove the breach.

## 8. Termination

Either party may terminate this agreement on written notice if the other party materially breaches it
and does not cure the breach within 30 days of written notice of it. The Licensor may terminate
immediately on written notice if the Partner does not pay after written notice that a payment is overdue,
or misuses the designation in item 4 of section 3.

A Seminar that is under way when termination takes effect may finish, and its Attendees' sublicences
last until its scheduled end. The designation and every other right granted by this agreement stop at
termination. Sections 5 and 6 continue to apply to Seminars and other Events held before termination or finished after
it, and sections 9 and 10 and any accrued payment obligation survive termination. The same applies, with the same in-flight rule, when the term
stated in Schedule A ends.
