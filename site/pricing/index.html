Editions & Commercial Strategy

A transparent approach to software sustainability. Clavity is currently in free public beta.

Status: Public Beta Evaluation. All core capabilities are free for noncommercial evaluation. Commercial pricing has not yet been established. We are currently conducting discovery research with engineering teams to validate commercial pilot requirements.

Proposed Dual-Edition Architecture

Community Edition

Free / Open Evaluation

Built for individual developers, open-source contributors, students, and independent researchers.

  • Full terminal pairing engine (clavity-dotnet and clavity-classic).
  • Adversarial Panel Review (adversarial-panel-review).
  • Pre-completion capstone code review (agy-capstone).
  • Mutation test suite audit (agy-test-audit).
  • Automatic repo defect capture and backlog triage.
  • Free noncommercial usage under PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0.

Install Free Beta

Commercial Edition

In Pilot Research

Designed for professional engineering teams, software consultancies, and enterprise codebases.

  • Commercial deployment rights (exempt from PolyForm noncommercial restriction).
  • Centralized quality policy enforcement across team repositories.
  • Automated CI/CD pull request verification bots.
  • Cryptographically signed review audit trails for compliance.
  • Shared organizational knowledge stores and cheatsheet pooling.
  • Dedicated engineering support and custom LLM peer onboarding.

Pricing Research & Pilot Participation

We do not publish artificial prices or enforce paywalls. If your engineering team is evaluating multi-agent code verification and requires commercial rights, centralized management, or CI/CD integration, we invite you to participate in our customer discovery interviews.

Read our research methodology in the repository: pricing research specification.
