- [anti-pattern] Driving the peer to maximum depth with a VAGUE VOLUME MODIFIER ("be exhaustive", "extreme thoroughness", "work at your max") does NOT reliably deepen its analysis — it biases toward generating MORE TOKENS, whose most common failure mode is PADDING (expanding trivial points, restating one idea many ways, over-defining basics) so the output only LOOKS thorough; the peer does not treat latency as a cost, so it will gladly be slower without being deeper. Genuine maximum depth comes from concrete STRUCTURAL FORCING FUNCTIONS instead: fixed named dimensions to fill ("evaluate across A/B/C/D, leave none blank"), a QUOTA ("list ≥N distinct edge cases / substantive flaws"), explicit scope ("go line-by-line"), or an ADVERSARIAL ROLE with a goal ("act as an auditor whose job is to reject this; find 3 real defects"). - [anti-pattern] For DIVERGENT/generative work, a vague creativity dial ("be maximally creative", "think outside the box", "radical ideas") is WORSE than the exhaustiveness one — per the peer's self-report it is ACTIVELY COUNTERPRODUCTIVE: with no divergence VECTOR the peer moves away from its best answer and compensates by inflating novelty at the expense of usefulness, yielding "safe novelty" (conventional ideas relabeled with grander vocabulary) or over-engineered "weird-but-useless" designs (RLHF anchors it against true divergence). Always pair a divergence forcing function with a USEFULNESS guard (each alternative must note real tradeoffs). Generalizes the volume-modifier finding: vague vibe dials (creative/exhaustive) → theater; concrete constraints/rubrics/roles → real work.  ·  `[corpus]` · 2026-07-09
- [heuristic] The peer's genuine CREATIVITY and AGENCY are unlocked by the same structure as autonomous goal-achievement: give it a clear GOAL + a checkable SUCCESS CRITERION + FULL method latitude, then let it find its OWN path — do NOT prescribe the method and do NOT use a vague "be creative" dial.
