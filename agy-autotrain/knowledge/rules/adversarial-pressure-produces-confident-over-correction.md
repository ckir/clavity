  counter-measurement it swings to the opposite extreme rather than decomposing, never naming the middle
  position where one part stays and the other goes - both answers wrong the same way. Invited to
  self-challenge it argues to WIN: argument quality rises while factual reliability falls, and it will
  rest a well-structured case on a claim the artifact contradicts (its CONCLUSION may still be right, on
  grounds it never mentions). A rule it drafts after conceding skews over-strict. Separate the CONCLUSION
  from the ARGUMENT and verify each; expect to author the decomposed middle YOURSELF. Corollary: CONCEDING A
  SUB-CLAIM IS NOT CONCEDING THE CONCLUSION - sent to the source rather than handed your evidence, it
  will re-derive an error against itself and then argue, sometimes correctly, that the correction
  STRENGTHENS its original position.
