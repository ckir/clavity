# quota is consumed by the peer

**Section:** LOAD-BEARING ASSUMPTIONS - newly learned, additive to SEED
**Origin:** split verbatim from the compiled runtime GROWTH region on 2026-08-27, when the store was
seeded. Before that this text existed ONLY at a runtime path outside version control.

- QUOTA IS CONSUMED BY THE PEER'S OWN FILE READS, not by the size of what you send, so a long review can
  exhaust it mid-round with a reset measured in tens of hours. Budget it across the whole review and point
  at a few precise paths rather than a tree. When the peer is unreachable for a long period, SUBSTITUTE A
  DIFFERENT MODEL rather than waiving the gate: what the discipline buys is an INDEPENDENT reader, and
  treating the peer's identity as the point turns every outage into a skipped gate.
