# Logo Assets Specification

Specifications and asset links for Clavity brand logos.

## Assets Inventory

1. **`site/assets/clavity.svg` (Primary Logo):**
   - Format: Vector SVG.
   - Design: The wordmark "clavity" positioned above an electric glowing bridge connecting an orange driver terminal on the left to a cyan peer terminal on the right.
   - Status: **Available & Shipped**.

2. **`site/assets/profile.png` (Brand Emblem):**
   - Format: Raster PNG (96x96 px, circular crop).
   - Design: Circular emblem featuring the neon bridge connector icon.
   - Status: **Available & Shipped**.

3. **Planned Variations for Production:**
   - `clavity-monochrome-dark.svg`: Pure white vector wordmark for high-contrast dark backgrounds.
   - `clavity-monochrome-light.svg`: Dark slate vector wordmark for light documentation prints.
   - `clavity-mark-only.svg`: Square standalone connector icon without wordmark for IDE icons and favicons.
