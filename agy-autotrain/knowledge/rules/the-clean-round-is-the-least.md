# the clean round is the least

**Section:** CRITICAL ANTI-PATTERNS - newly learned, additive to SEED
**Origin:** split verbatim from the compiled runtime GROWTH region on 2026-08-27, when the store was
seeded. Before that this text existed ONLY at a runtime path outside version control.

- THE CLEAN ROUND IS THE LEAST-GUARDED REPLY YOU GET, AND IT CAN LAUNDER A DEFECT THE PEER ITSELF FOUND.
  A per-finding quote rule has no findings to bite on, so citations migrate into the "where I looked"
  prose where nothing checks them - treat those as findings-citations. Worse, measured twice now: every
  seat reported clean while the SAME report's below-floor list and direct answers named a real defect,
  filed as a procedural note instead of a finding. NEVER accept a clean verdict without reading the
  discarded-below-floor items and every direct answer, and say in advance that those will be read as
  carefully as the findings. A clean round whose citations ALL verify can still fail by OMISSION: it
  reasons thoroughly about what you pointed it at and never asks what ELSE would break its conclusion.
  SCOPE is the lever - a round NARROWED to one changed location, carrying a do-not-re-raise ledger and an
  explicit licence to declare it clean, returned an honest no-findings verdict whose unprompted citations
  were ALL correct. Probing works too - N numbered "quote line N verbatim, or reply DOES NOT EXIST" plus a
  line PAST end-of-file as a control - but a probe pass credits the READING, never the numbers.
